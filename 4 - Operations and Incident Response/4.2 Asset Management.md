#### Acquisition/Procurement Process
- The purchasing process is usually multi step for requesting and obtaining goods/services
- Start w/ a request from the user
	- Usually includes budgeting info and formal approvals
- Negotiate with suppliers
	- Terms and conditions
- Purchase, invoice, and payment
#### Accounting
- A central asset tracking system
	- Used by all parts of the organization
- Ownership
	- Associate a person w/ an asset
	- Useful for tracking a system
- Classification
	- Type of asset
	- hardware or software?
#### Monitoring / asset tracking
- Inventory every asset
	- Laptops, desktops, servers, etc, and all associated hardware components
- Associate a support ticket with a device make and model
	- Can be more detailed than a user's description
- Enumeration
	- List all parts of an asset
	- CPU, memory, storage, keyboard, mouse, etc.
- Add an asset tag like a Barcode, RFID, org name, tracking number
#### Media Sanitization
- System disposal or decommissioning
	- Remove all data
- Different use cases
	- Clean hard drive for future use
	- Delete single files, or the entire system
- A one-way trip, no recovery with forensics tools
- Reuse the storage media
#### Physical Destruction
- Shredder or pulverizer
	- Heavy machinery for max destruction
- Drill / Hammer
	- Quick and easy, go through all platters
- Electromagnetic (degaussing)
	- Remove the magnetic field of drive, renders drive unusable and unreadable
#### Certification of destruction
- Often destruction is done by a 3rd party
- They give a confirmation that your data is destroyed, called a **certificate of destruction**
- A paper trail of broken data, you know what happened
#### Data retention
- Backup your data, but how much and where?
- Regulatory compliance
	- Certain amount of data backup is required
	- Emails, corporate financial data
- Operational needs
	- Accidental deletion
	- Disaster recovery
- Differentiate by type and application
	- Recover the right data when you need it
