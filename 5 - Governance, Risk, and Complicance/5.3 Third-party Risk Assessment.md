#### Third-party risk
- Every organization works with vendors
	- Payroll, customer relationship management, email marketing, travel, etc.
- Important company data is often shared
- Perform a risk assessment
	- Categorize risk by vendor and manage it
- Use contracts for clean understanding, put the risk assessment info into to the contract so everyone understands
	- Use it to force a secure enviornment
#### Penetration testing
- Pentest
	- Simulate an attack
- Similar to vulnerability scanning
	- Except we actually try and exploit vulnerabilites
- Often a compliance mandate
- Regular penetration testing by a 3rd-party
#### Rules of engagement
- An important documents
	- Defines purpose and scope of the pentest
- Type of testing and schedule
	- On-site physical breach, internal test, external test
	- Normal working hours, after 6pm only, etc.
- The rules
	- IP address ranges
	- emergency contacts
	- How to handle sensitive info
	- in-scope and out of scope devices / apps
#### Right-to-audit clauses
- Common to work with business partners
	- Data sharing
	- Outsourcing
- Third party can hold all of the data on an internet provider or in a facility
- So, a right-to-audit should be in the contract
	- A legal agreement to have the option to perform a security audit at any time
	- Everyone agrees to the terms and conditions
#### Evidence of internal audits
- Evaluate the effectiveness of security controls
	- Done by a third-party
- May be required for compliance
- Check for security controls and processes
	- Access management, off boarding, password security, VPN controls, etc.
- Perform at a reasonable frequency
#### Supply chain analysis
- The system involved when creating a product
	- Involves organizations, people, activities, and resources
- Supply chain analysis
	- Get a product or service from supplier to customer
	- Evaluate coordination between groups
	- ID areas of improvement
	- Assess the IT systems support the operation
	- Document the business process changes
#### Independent assessments
- Bring in a smart person or team to evaluate security and provide recommendations
- Specialists in their field
	- They do this all day every day for many organizations
	- Can provide options you may not have considered
#### Vendor selection process
- Due diligence
	- Check a company out before doing business
	- Investigate and verify information
	- Financial status, pending or past legal issues, etc.
	- Background checks, personnel interviews
- Conflict of interest
	- A personal interest could compromise judgement
		- A potential partner also does businesss with your biggest competitor
#### Vendor monitoring
- Ongoing management of the vendor relationship
- Reviews should occur on a regular basis
	- Financial health check, etc.
- Different vendors may be checked for different indicators
	- Quantitative and qualitative analysis
- Assign a person to be in charge of the vendor relationship to manage the monitoring process
#### Questionnaires
- An important of due diligence and onging vendor monitoring
- Security-related questions
	- What plans are in place for disaster recovery?
	- What secure storage method is used for company data? etc.

## Agreement Types
- Service Level Agreement (SLA)
	- Minimum terms the services provided
	- Uptime, response time agreement, etc.
	- Commonly used between customers and service providers
- Contract with an Internet provider
	- SLA is no more than four hours of unscheduled downtime
	- Technician will be dispatched
	- May require customer to keep spare equipment on-site
- Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
	- Both sides agree in general to the contents of the memorandum
	- Usually states common goals, but not much more
	- Very informal, more a broad overview
- Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)
	- One step above the MOU
	- Describes in more detail the relationship between the two organizations
	- Can have legally binding info, but is not a contract and doesn't provide legal enforcement
- Master Service Agreement (MSA)
	- Legal contract and agreement of terms
	- A broad framework to cover later transactions
	- Many detailed negotiations happen here
- Work order (WO) / Statement of Work (SOW)
	- Specific list of items to be completed
	- Used in conjunction with a MSA
	- Details the scope of the job, location, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and more
	- Was job done properly? Refer to the SOW
#### Non-disclosure agreement (NDA)
- Confidentiality agreement between parties
- Protect confidential information
	- Trade secrets
	- Business activities
	- Anything else listed in the NDA
- Can be unilateral or bilateral (or multilateral)
- Formal contract w/ signatures requried
#### More common agreements
- Business Partners Agreement (BPA)
	- Going into business together
	- Owner stake
	- A financial contract
	- Also address decision-making
