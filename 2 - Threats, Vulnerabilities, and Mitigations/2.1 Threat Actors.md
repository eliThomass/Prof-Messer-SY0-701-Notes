- The entity responsible for an event that has an impact on the safety of another entity
- Threat actor attributes
- Useful to categorize the motivation

#### Attributes of threat actors
- Internal/external
- Resources/funding
- Level of sophistication/capability

#### Motivations of threat actors
- Data exfilration
- Espoinage
- Service disruption
- Blackmail
- Finanical gain
- Philopshopical/political belifefs
- Revenge
- War
- Lots more...

#### Nation states
- External entity
- Many motivations
	- Such as data exfiltration, philoshopical, revenge, disruption, war
- Constant attacks, massive resources, highest sophistication
	- Commonly an **Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)**

#### Unskilled attackers
- Runs pre-made scripts without any knowledge of what's really happening
- If it doesn't work, they don't how to fix it
- Motivated by the hunt
- Usually an external entity
- Low funding, low sophistication

#### Hacktivist
- A hacker with a purpose
- Often an external entity
	- Can be hired by the org they attack
- Can be very sophisticated and use very specific hacks
- Limited funding (usually)

#### Insider threat
- Extensive resources, use the org's own resources against themselves
- An internal entity
- Medium level of sophistication
	- Institutional knowledge

#### Organized crime
- Professional criminals
- Very sophisticated & funded
- Crime that's organized
	- One person hacks, one sells data, one manages exploits...

#### Shadow IT
- Group or dept. that works around rules put in place by the IT dept. of a company.
- Shadow IT is unencumbered
- Limited resources
- Medium sophistication, may/may not have IT training
