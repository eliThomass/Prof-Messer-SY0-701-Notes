# Quiz 5.3 — Third-party Risk Assessment
*Professor Messer SY0-701 · Section 5.3*

---

### 1. Why do organizations need to assess third-party risk?
- [ ] Vendors are legally required to share their passwords
- [ ] Important company data is often shared with vendors
- [ ] Vendors always run the organization's own firewalls
- [ ] Third parties can never be held to a written contract

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Important company data is often shared with vendors</p>
<p>Every organization works with vendors, like payroll, CRM, email marketing, and travel. Categorize risk by vendor and manage it.</p>
</details>

---

### 2. How can a contract help manage third-party risk?
- [ ] By removing the need for any further vendor monitoring
- [ ] By transferring all legal liability to the vendor
- [ ] By including the risk assessment to force a secure environment
- [ ] By letting the vendor decide its own security controls

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> By including the risk assessment to force a secure environment</p>
<p>Putting the risk assessment info into the contract gives everyone a clear understanding.</p>
</details>

---

### 3. How does a penetration test differ from a vulnerability scan?
- [ ] A pentest actually tries to exploit the vulnerabilities
- [ ] A pentest only looks for missing patches on a schedule
- [ ] A pentest is never part of any compliance mandate
- [ ] A pentest can only be performed by internal staff

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A pentest actually tries to exploit the vulnerabilities</p>
<p>A pentest simulates an attack. It's often a compliance mandate, with regular testing done by a third party.</p>
</details>

---

### 4. Which document defines the purpose and scope of a penetration test?
- [ ] Right-to-audit clause
- [ ] Rules of engagement
- [ ] Statement of work
- [ ] Non-disclosure agreement

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Rules of engagement</p>
<p>It also covers the type of testing and schedule, like on-site physical breach, internal, or external tests during set hours.</p>
</details>

---

### 5. Which items are part of a pentest's rules of engagement?
- [ ] Owner stake, financial terms, and decision-making
- [ ] Uptime guarantees and technician response times
- [ ] IP address ranges and emergency contacts
- [ ] Trade secrets and other business activities

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> IP address ranges and emergency contacts</p>
<p>The rules also cover how to handle sensitive info and which devices and apps are in or out of scope.</p>
</details>

---

### 6. A business partner holds your data in its own facility. What contract clause lets you perform a security audit at any time?
- [ ] Non-disclosure clause
- [ ] Service level clause
- [ ] Conflict of interest clause
- [ ] Right-to-audit clause

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Right-to-audit clause</p>
<p>It's a legal agreement that everyone signs on to. It's common when working with partners for data sharing or outsourcing.</p>
</details>

---

### 7. What do internal audits evaluate?
- [ ] The effectiveness of security controls
- [ ] The vendor's pending legal issues
- [ ] The financial stake of each owner
- [ ] The uptime promised by the ISP

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> The effectiveness of security controls</p>
<p>Audits check controls and processes like access management, offboarding, password security, and VPN controls. They may be required for compliance and should happen at a reasonable frequency.</p>
</details>

---

### 8. Which is part of a supply chain analysis?
- [ ] Signing a confidentiality agreement with each supplier
- [ ] Simulating an attack against the supplier's network
- [ ] Evaluating coordination between groups in the chain
- [ ] Setting a vendor's maximum unscheduled downtime

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Evaluating coordination between groups in the chain</p>
<p>The supply chain is the system of organizations, people, activities, and resources involved in getting a product from supplier to customer. The analysis also identifies improvements, assesses the IT systems that support it, and documents business process changes.</p>
</details>

---

### 9. What is a benefit of an independent assessment?
- [ ] It's always cheaper than doing the work internally
- [ ] It replaces the need for any internal security staff
- [ ] It's legally binding on every vendor you work with
- [ ] Specialists can offer options you hadn't considered

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Specialists can offer options you hadn't considered</p>
<p>Independent assessors do this all day for many organizations, so they bring broad experience when evaluating security and making recommendations.</p>
</details>

---

### 10. Checking a vendor's financial status and past legal issues before doing business is called what?
- [ ] Due diligence
- [ ] Vendor monitoring
- [ ] Supply chain analysis
- [ ] Right to audit

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Due diligence</p>
<p>Due diligence means investigating and verifying information, including background checks and personnel interviews.</p>
</details>

---

### 11. A potential partner also does business with your biggest competitor. What concern does this raise?
- [ ] A right-to-audit issue
- [ ] A conflict of interest
- [ ] A supply chain gap
- [ ] A rules of engagement issue

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A conflict of interest</p>
<p>A personal interest could compromise judgment.</p>
</details>

---

### 12. Which is part of ongoing vendor monitoring?
- [ ] One review at signing, then never again
- [ ] Regular reviews like financial health checks
- [ ] Checking every vendor for the same indicators
- [ ] Letting each vendor manage its own reviews

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Regular reviews like financial health checks</p>
<p>Different vendors may be checked for different indicators with quantitative and qualitative analysis. Assign one person to manage the vendor relationship.</p>
</details>

---

### 13. "What plans are in place for disaster recovery?" would most likely appear in which document?
- [ ] A memorandum of understanding
- [ ] A business partners agreement
- [ ] A vendor security questionnaire
- [ ] A rules of engagement document

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A vendor security questionnaire</p>
<p>Questionnaires are an important part of due diligence and ongoing vendor monitoring. Another example question is how company data is securely stored.</p>
</details>

---

### 14. An ISP contract promises no more than four hours of unscheduled downtime. Which agreement type is this?
- [ ] MOU
- [ ] SLA
- [ ] BPA
- [ ] NDA

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> SLA</p>
<p>A service level agreement sets the minimum terms for services provided, like uptime and response time. It's common between customers and service providers and may require the customer to keep spare equipment on-site.</p>
</details>

---

### 15. Which agreement is very informal, with both sides agreeing to common goals but not much more?
- [ ] MOA
- [ ] MSA
- [ ] SOW
- [ ] MOU

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> MOU</p>
<p>A memorandum of understanding is more of a broad overview.</p>
</details>

---

### 16. What is true about a memorandum of agreement (MOA)?
- [ ] It's a legal contract that covers all later transactions
- [ ] It's more detailed than an MOU, but it isn't a contract
- [ ] It lists the specific deliverables for a single job
- [ ] It's an informal statement of common goals only

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> It's more detailed than an MOU, but it isn't a contract</p>
<p>An MOA describes the relationship in more detail and can contain legally binding info, but it doesn't provide legal enforcement.</p>
</details>

---

### 17. Which agreement is a legal contract that creates a broad framework for later transactions?
- [ ] MSA
- [ ] MOA
- [ ] SLA
- [ ] BPA

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> MSA</p>
<p>Many detailed negotiations happen in the master service agreement.</p>
</details>

---

### 18. A job is finished, and you need to check whether it was done properly. Which document should you refer to?
- [ ] The memorandum of understanding
- [ ] The non-disclosure agreement
- [ ] The business partners agreement
- [ ] The statement of work

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> The statement of work</p>
<p>A statement of work (or work order) lists the specific items to complete, with the scope, location, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.</p>
</details>

---

### 19. A statement of work (SOW) is commonly used together with which agreement?
- [ ] MOU
- [ ] MSA
- [ ] NDA
- [ ] SLA

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> MSA</p>
<p>The MSA sets the broad framework, and each SOW details a specific job under it.</p>
</details>

---

### 20. Which statement about a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is true?
- [ ] It's informal and doesn't need any signatures
- [ ] It can only protect one side's information
- [ ] It's a formal contract that can be unilateral or bilateral
- [ ] It sets the uptime that a service provider guarantees

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> It's a formal contract that can be unilateral or bilateral</p>
<p>An NDA is a confidentiality agreement that protects trade secrets, business activities, and anything else it lists. It can also be multilateral.</p>
</details>

---

### 21. Two companies go into business together and need to set owner stakes and decision-making. Which agreement do they need?
- [ ] BPA
- [ ] SOW
- [ ] SLA
- [ ] MOU

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> BPA</p>
<p>A business partners agreement is a financial contract that covers ownership stakes and decision-making.</p>
</details>

---

### 22. Which list goes from least formal to most formal?
- [ ] MSA, MOA, MOU
- [ ] MOA, MOU, MSA
- [ ] MOU, MSA, MOA
- [ ] MOU, MOA, MSA

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> MOU, MOA, MSA</p>
<p>An MOU is informal, an MOA is one step above it with more detail but no legal enforcement, and an MSA is a legal contract.</p>
</details>
