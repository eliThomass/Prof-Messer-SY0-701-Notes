# Quiz 2.1 — Threat Actors
*Professor Messer SY0-701 · Section 2.1*

---

### 1. Which three attributes are commonly used to categorize a threat actor?
- [ ] Age, nationality, and job title
- [ ] Internal/external, resources/funding, and level of sophistication/capability
- [ ] Motivation, salary, and education level
- [ ] Location, hardware used, and preferred programming language

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Internal/external, resources/funding, and level of sophistication/capability</p>
<p>Threat actor attributes are useful for categorizing motivation, and include whether they're internal or external, their resources/funding, and their level of sophistication/capability.</p>
</details>

---

### 2. Which threat actor type is an external entity with constant attacks, massive resources, and the highest sophistication, commonly referred to as an Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)?
- [ ] Hacktivist
- [ ] Nation state
- [ ] Shadow IT
- [ ] Unskilled attacker

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Nation state</p>
<p>Nation states are external entities with many motivations (data exfiltration, philosophical, revenge, disruption, war), constant attacks, massive resources, and the highest sophistication — commonly an APT.</p>
</details>

---

### 3. Which threat actor runs pre-made scripts without understanding what's really happening, and can't fix it if it doesn't work?
- [ ] Organized crime
- [ ] Insider threat
- [ ] Unskilled attacker
- [ ] Hacktivist

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Unskilled attacker</p>
<p>Unskilled attackers run pre-made scripts without any knowledge of what's really happening, are usually external, motivated by the hunt, and have low funding and low sophistication.</p>
</details>

---

### 4. Which threat actor is described as "a hacker with a purpose," often external, and can even be hired by the organization they end up attacking?
- [ ] Hacktivist
- [ ] Shadow IT
- [ ] Nation state
- [ ] Insider threat

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Hacktivist</p>
<p>A hacktivist is a hacker with a purpose, often an external entity (sometimes hired by the org they attack), can be very sophisticated, but usually has limited funding.</p>
</details>

---

### 5. Which threat actor is an internal entity that uses the organization's own resources against itself, leveraging institutional knowledge?
- [ ] Insider threat
- [ ] Organized crime
- [ ] Unskilled attacker
- [ ] Nation state

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Insider threat</p>
<p>Insider threats are internal entities with extensive resources, using the org's own resources against itself, with a medium level of sophistication built on institutional knowledge.</p>
</details>

---

### 6. Which threat actor operates like a business, with distinct roles such as one person hacking, one selling data, and one managing exploits?
- [ ] Organized crime
- [ ] Shadow IT
- [ ] Hacktivist
- [ ] Unskilled attacker

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Organized crime</p>
<p>Organized crime consists of professional criminals — very sophisticated and well-funded, with crime that's organized into specialized roles.</p>
</details>

---

### 7. Which threat actor refers to a group or department within a company that works around rules put in place by the IT department?
- [ ] Shadow IT
- [ ] Insider threat
- [ ] Hacktivist
- [ ] Nation state

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Shadow IT</p>
<p>Shadow IT is a group or department that works around the rules put in place by the IT department — unencumbered, with limited resources and medium sophistication (may or may not have IT training).</p>
</details>

- [ ] Unskilled attacker

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

### 9. Which threat actor is rated as having "very low" sophistication and "limited" resources in the attribute table?
- [ ] Nation state
- [ ] Unskilled attacker
- [ ] Insider threat
- [ ] Organized crime

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Unskilled attacker</p>
<p>The unskilled attacker is external, has limited resources, and very low sophistication, motivated by disruption, data exfiltration, or philosophical beliefs.</p>
</details>

### 10. What does the acronym APT stand for?
- [ ] Automated Penetration Test
- [ ] Advanced Persistent Threat
- [ ] Active Protocol Trace
- [ ] Authorized Program Terminal

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Advanced Persistent Threat</p>
<p>Nation states are commonly referred to as an Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) due to their constant attacks, massive resources, and highest level of sophistication.</p>
</details>

---

### 11. Which threat actor is classified as "internal" in the attribute table but has "many resources" and only "limited" sophistication?
- [ ] Insider threat
- [ ] Shadow IT
- [ ] Nation state
- [ ] Hacktivist

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> Shadow IT</p>
<p>Shadow IT is internal, has many resources (access to company systems/budget) but limited sophistication, motivated by philosophical beliefs or revenge.</p>
</details>

- [ ] Blackmail
