# Quiz 5.1 — Security Policies
*Professor Messer SY0-701 · Section 5.1*

---

### 1. Security policies answer which questions, while technical security controls answer "how"?
- [ ] "Who" and "where"
- [ ] "What" and "why"
- [ ] "When" and "where"
- [ ] "Who" and "when"

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> "What" and "why"</p>
<p>Policies set the rules everyone follows to provide confidentiality, integrity, and availability, from high-level strategies to detailed security goals.</p>
</details>

---

### 2. What must happen for an information security policy to actually work?
- [ ] It must be written by an outside auditor
- [ ] It must list every firewall rule in detail
- [ ] The organization has to actually enforce it
- [ ] It must be kept secret from the employees

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> The organization has to actually enforce it</p>
<p>The information security policy is the big list of all security-related policies: compliance requirements, detailed procedures, and roles and responsibilities. On its own, it's just words.</p>
</details>

---

### 3. Why does an organization create an acceptable use policy (AUP)?
- [ ] To limit the organization's legal liability
- [ ] To define the order of its firewall rules
- [ ] To pick a recovery location for disasters
- [ ] To choose its data encryption algorithms

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>
<p><strong>Answer:</strong> To limit the organization's legal liability</p>
<p>An AUP documents acceptable use of company assets, covering Internet use, telephones, computers, mobile devices, and more.</p>
</details>

### 13. Which belongs in a password standard?

- [ ] Policies for secure password resets

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

### 15. Which would appear in a physical security standard?
